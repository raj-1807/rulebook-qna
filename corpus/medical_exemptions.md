# Greenfield University — Medical Exemptions and Special Accommodations Policy

*Effective Date: August 1, 2025*
*Issued by the Office of the Dean of Student Affairs*
*Reference: Academic Council Resolution AC-2025-021*

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes procedures for granting medical exemptions and special accommodations to students whose ability to fulfill standard academic requirements is impaired by documented medical conditions or disabilities.

---

## 2. Attendance Exemptions

### 2.1 Medical Attendance Exemption

Students who are unable to meet the standard attendance requirement of 75% (as specified in the General Academic Regulations, Section 3.1) due to a documented medical condition may apply for a reduced attendance requirement. Upon approval, the minimum attendance threshold shall be reduced to **60%** for the affected courses during the period of the medical condition.

### 2.2 Application Procedure

To apply for a medical attendance exemption, the student must submit:
1. A completed Medical Exemption Application Form (Form ME-01).
2. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner specifying:
   - The nature of the condition (without requiring disclosure of specific diagnosis if the student prefers confidentiality).
   - The expected duration of the condition.
   - The recommended accommodations.
3. Supporting documentation from the treating hospital or clinic.

Applications must be submitted within fifteen (15) calendar days of the onset of the condition or the beginning of the semester, whichever is later.

### 2.3 Approval Authority

Medical attendance exemptions are approved by the Dean of Student Affairs in consultation with the University Medical Officer. The decision shall be communicated within ten (10) working days of receiving a complete application.

### 2.4 Limitations on Medical Attendance Exemption

Medical attendance exemptions are subject to the following restrictions:
- The reduced attendance requirement of 60% is the absolute minimum; **attendance may not be waived below 60% under any circumstances**.
- A medical exemption does not excuse the student from completing all graded coursework.
- The exemption is valid for the period specified in the medical documentation, up to a maximum of one (1) academic year.
- Students requiring exemption beyond one year must submit a fresh application.

### 2.5 Laboratory and Practical Courses

For courses with a significant laboratory or practical component (more than 30% of total contact hours), the minimum attendance for the practical component shall be **70%** even under a medical exemption, unless the course can be assessed through an alternative practical examination approved by the Head of Department.

## 3. Examination Accommodations

### 3.1 Types of Examination Accommodations

Students with documented medical conditions or disabilities may be granted the following examination accommodations:
- Extended time (25% to 100% additional time, depending on the condition).
- Use of a separate examination room.
- Use of assistive technology (screen readers, magnifiers, etc.).
- Permission for a scribe or reader.
- Modified examination format (oral instead of written, or vice versa).
- Scheduled breaks during examinations.

### 3.2 Application for Examination Accommodations

Applications for examination accommodations must be submitted at least thirty (30) days before the commencement of examinations. The application must include:
- Medical or disability documentation from a qualified specialist.
- A recommendation from the University Disability Services Coordinator.

### 3.3 Temporary Medical Conditions

For temporary medical conditions (e.g., fractures, acute illness, post-surgical recovery), accommodations may be granted on an emergency basis with a shorter notice period of seven (7) days.

## 4. Mental Health Accommodations

### 4.1 Recognition of Mental Health Conditions

Greenfield University recognizes that mental health conditions, including anxiety disorders, depression, ADHD, and other diagnosed conditions, may significantly impact a student's academic performance and attendance.

### 4.2 Confidentiality

All mental health documentation is handled with the highest level of confidentiality. Information is shared only with individuals directly involved in the accommodation process and only to the extent necessary.

### 4.3 Academic Adjustments

Students with documented mental health conditions may receive:
- Deadline extensions for assignments (typically 3-5 additional days).
- Permission to reschedule examinations during acute episodes.
- Reduced course load below the minimum requirement for up to two (2) semesters.
- Priority access to counseling services at the University Health Center.

### 4.4 Crisis Protocol

If a student experiences a mental health crisis:
1. The University Health Center crisis line (extension 1100) is available 24/7.
2. Faculty members who observe signs of distress should contact the Dean of Student Affairs.
3. The student may be granted an immediate temporary leave of absence without the standard 30-day advance notice requirement.

---

## 5. Pregnancy and Parental Accommodations

### 5.1 Non-Discrimination

No student shall face academic penalty solely due to pregnancy, childbirth, or related conditions.

### 5.2 Academic Accommodations

Pregnant students and students who have recently given birth may receive:
- Attendance exemption for a period of up to twelve (12) weeks.
- Extension of assignment and examination deadlines.
- Permission to complete the semester's remaining coursework in the following semester without re-registration.
- Modified laboratory participation requirements when necessary for safety.

### 5.3 Application Process

Students should notify the Dean of Student Affairs as early as possible. Accommodations shall be arranged in consultation with the student, the relevant faculty members, and the University Medical Officer.

---

## 6. Disability Services

### 6.1 Registration with Disability Services

Students with permanent or long-term disabilities are encouraged to register with the University Disability Services Office at the beginning of their enrollment. Registration allows for proactive accommodation planning.

### 6.2 Infrastructure Accessibility

The university is committed to maintaining accessible infrastructure, including:
- Wheelchair-accessible classrooms and examination halls.
- Accessible hostel rooms on the ground floor.
- Accessible library facilities with assistive technology stations.
- Accessible laboratory facilities or alternative arrangements.

### 6.3 Academic Accommodations for Students with Disabilities

Students with registered disabilities may receive accommodations including but not limited to:
- Extended time on all examinations and assessments.
- Note-taking assistance.
- Audio recordings of lectures (with faculty permission).
- Priority course registration to ensure accessible classroom assignments.
- Flexible attendance requirements determined on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 7. Appeals

### 7.1 Right to Appeal

Students whose accommodation requests are denied may appeal to the Academic Committee within fifteen (15) calendar days of receiving the denial. The Academic Committee shall review the appeal and may consult with external medical experts if necessary.

### 7.2 Interim Accommodations

While an appeal is pending, the student may request interim accommodations from the Dean of Student Affairs. Interim accommodations are temporary and do not prejudice the outcome of the appeal.

---

## 8. Review and Oversight

### 8.1 Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Student Welfare Committee in consultation with the Disability Services Office, the University Health Center, and student representatives.

### 8.2 Data Collection

The Dean of Student Affairs shall maintain anonymized records of all accommodation requests, approvals, and denials for the purpose of policy improvement and compliance reporting. These records shall not include identifying student information.
