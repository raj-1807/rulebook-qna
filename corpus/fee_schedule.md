# Greenfield University — Fee Schedule and Financial Regulations

*Academic Year 2025–2026*
*Issued by the Office of Finance, June 15, 2025*

---

## 1. Tuition and Fees

### 1.1 Fee Structure

The following fees are applicable per semester for all enrolled students:

| Fee Category | Amount (₹) | Normal Deadline | Late Deadline | Late Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tuition Fee | 85,000 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹5,000 |
| Laboratory Fee | 12,000 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹1,500 |
| Library Fee | 3,000 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹500 |
| Technology Fee | 5,000 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹750 |
| Hostel Fee (if applicable) | 45,000 | August 5 | August 15 | ₹3,000 |
| Mess Fee (if applicable) | 30,000 | August 5 | August 15 | ₹2,000 |
| Student Activity Fee | 2,500 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹400 |
| Examination Fee | 4,000 | August 10 | August 20 | ₹600 |

*Note: Spring semester deadlines follow the same structure with January dates.*

### 1.2 Payment Methods

Fees may be paid through:
- Online bank transfer to the University account.
- Demand draft payable to "Greenfield University."
- UPI payment through the University Portal.
- Credit/Debit card through the University Payment Gateway (2% convenience fee applies for credit cards).

### 1.3 Fee Payment Confirmation

Payment confirmation shall be available on the University Portal within three (3) business days of payment. Students must retain proof of payment until confirmation is visible on the portal.

---

## 2. Late Payment Policy

### 2.1 Late Fees

Students who fail to pay fees by the normal deadline but pay before the late deadline shall incur the late penalty specified in the fee table above.

### 2.2 Non-Payment

Students who fail to pay all required fees by the late deadline shall have their registration suspended. A suspended student:
- May not attend classes.
- May not access laboratory facilities.
- May not appear in examinations.
- Will not receive grades for the semester.

### 2.3 Reinstatement

A student whose registration has been suspended due to non-payment may be reinstated upon payment of all outstanding fees plus a reinstatement fee of ₹10,000, provided the reinstatement occurs within four (4) weeks of the suspension date. After four weeks, the student must re-register for the following semester.

## 3. Refund Policy

### 3.1 Withdrawal Refund Schedule

Refunds for students who officially withdraw from the university shall be calculated as follows:

| Withdrawal Timing | Refund Percentage |
|---|---|
| Before classes begin | 90% of tuition |
| Within first 2 weeks of instruction | 75% of tuition |
| Within first 4 weeks of instruction | 50% of tuition |
| Within first 6 weeks of instruction | 25% of tuition |
| After 6 weeks of instruction | No refund |

*Note: Non-refundable fees include the examination fee and the student activity fee.*

### 3.2 Medical Withdrawal Refund

Students who withdraw due to a documented medical condition may receive a **full refund of tuition** regardless of the withdrawal date, subject to approval by the Dean of Student Affairs and submission of medical documentation from a recognized hospital. The medical documentation must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the withdrawal date.

### 3.3 Processing Time

Approved refunds shall be processed within forty-five (45) business days of the approval date. Refunds shall be credited to the same account from which the original payment was made.

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid

### 4.1 Merit Scholarships

Merit scholarships are awarded based on academic performance:

| Scholarship Tier | CGPA Requirement | Award (% of Tuition) |
|---|---|---|
| Chancellor's Gold | 9.5 and above | 100% |
| Dean's Silver | 9.0 – 9.49 | 75% |
| Academic Excellence | 8.5 – 8.99 | 50% |
| Merit Recognition | 8.0 – 8.49 | 25% |

### 4.2 Scholarship Eligibility

To be eligible for a merit scholarship, a student must:
1. Have completed at least two (2) semesters at Greenfield University.
2. Have no pending disciplinary actions.
3. Maintain a **minimum attendance of 85%** across all courses.
4. Be registered as a full-time student carrying the minimum required course load.
5. Not be on academic probation.

### 4.3 Scholarship Renewal

Scholarships are renewed each semester based on the previous semester's CGPA. If a student's CGPA falls below the threshold for their current scholarship tier, the scholarship shall be:
- Reduced to the appropriate lower tier, if eligible.
- Revoked entirely if the CGPA falls below 8.0.

### 4.4 Need-Based Financial Aid

Students demonstrating financial need may apply for need-based financial aid through the Financial Aid Office. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and require:
- Completed financial aid application form.
- Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian(s).
- Bank statements for the preceding twelve (12) months.
- Any other documentation requested by the Financial Aid Office.

### 4.5 Emergency Financial Assistance

Students facing sudden financial hardship due to unforeseen circumstances (medical emergency, natural disaster, loss of family income) may apply for emergency financial assistance. The Emergency Aid Committee may grant:
- Deferment of fee payment for up to one (1) semester.
- Partial fee waiver of up to 50% of tuition for one semester.
- Interest-free loan to be repaid within twelve (12) months of graduation.

## 5. Special Fees

### 5.1 Course Repetition Fee

Students repeating a course shall pay a per-credit fee of ₹3,000 per credit hour, in addition to regular tuition.

### 5.2 Transcript Fee

Official transcript: ₹500 per copy.
Expedited transcript (within 3 business days): ₹1,000 per copy.

### 5.3 Convocation Fee

Convocation attendance fee: ₹2,000 (one-time, payable in the final semester).
Duplicate degree certificate: ₹5,000.

### 5.4 International Student Surcharge

International students (non-Indian citizens or Persons of Indian Origin without OCI card) shall pay an additional surcharge of 25% on tuition fees. This surcharge is not eligible for merit scholarship coverage.
