# Greenfield University — Hostel Regulations and Student Conduct Code

*Effective Date: August 1, 2025*
*Approved by the Student Welfare Committee, Resolution SWC-2025-008*

---

## 1. Hostel Allocation

### 1.1 Eligibility

On-campus hostel accommodation is available to all full-time students enrolled in degree programs. Priority allocation is based on:
1. Distance of permanent residence from the university (students from beyond 100 km receive first priority).
2. Year of study (first-year students receive second priority).
3. Academic merit (CGPA-based ranking for remaining seats).

### 1.2 Allocation Process

Hostel allocation for the academic year is conducted in June. Students must apply through the University Portal by June 15. Allocation results are published by July 1. Students who receive allocation must confirm their acceptance and pay the hostel fee by the deadline specified in the Fee Schedule.

### 1.3 Room Assignment

- First-year students are assigned shared rooms (2 students per room).
- Second-year and above students may request single occupancy rooms, subject to availability.
- Room assignments are final for the semester. Room changes are permitted only with approval from the Hostel Warden and only during the first two weeks of the semester.

## 2. Hostel Rules

### 2.1 Timings

- Hostel entry gate closes at **10:00 PM** on weekdays and **11:00 PM** on weekends and holidays.
- Students must be inside the hostel premises by gate closing time.
- Emergency exit after gate closing requires written permission from the Hostel Warden.

### 2.2 Visitors

- Visitors are permitted in the hostel common areas only between **4:00 PM and 8:00 PM**.
- No overnight guests are permitted under any circumstances.
- All visitors must register at the hostel reception desk with a valid photo ID.

### 2.3 Property and Maintenance

- Students are responsible for maintaining their assigned rooms in good condition.
- Any damage to hostel property shall be charged to the student(s) responsible at replacement cost plus a 20% administrative surcharge.
- Alterations to room fixtures, walls, or electrical fittings are prohibited.

### 2.4 Prohibited Items

The following items are strictly prohibited in hostel premises:
- Alcoholic beverages and intoxicating substances.
- Weapons of any kind.
- Cooking appliances (except electric kettles up to 1 liter capacity).
- Pets and animals.
- Loudspeakers and amplified audio equipment.
- Fireworks and combustible materials.

### 2.5 Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are observed from **10:00 PM to 7:00 AM** daily. During quiet hours, students must maintain noise levels that do not disturb other residents. Repeated violations of quiet hours may result in disciplinary action.

### 3.1 Meal Timings

| Meal | Weekday | Weekend |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM | 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM |
| Lunch | 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM | 1:00 PM – 2:30 PM |
| Dinner | 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM | 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM |

### 3.2 Mess Fee

The mess fee is mandatory for all hostel residents. Students who wish to opt out of the mess facility must submit a written request to the Hostel Warden at least two (2) weeks before the start of the semester. Opt-out requests are granted only in cases of documented medical dietary requirements that the mess cannot accommodate.

### 3.3 Guest Meals

Hostel residents may bring guests for meals by purchasing guest meal coupons at ₹150 per meal from the hostel reception. A maximum of three (3) guest meals per student per week is permitted.

## 4. Disciplinary Rules

### 4.1 Code of Conduct

All students of Greenfield University are expected to conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the values of academic integrity, mutual respect, and responsible citizenship. The following behaviors constitute violations of the Code of Conduct:

**Category A — Major Violations:**
- Physical assault or threat of violence.
- Sexual harassment or misconduct.
- Possession or distribution of illegal substances.
- Theft or destruction of university property.
- Ragging in any form (as defined under UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations).
- Forgery of documents or impersonation.

**Category B — Moderate Violations:**
- Verbal abuse or intimidation.
- Unauthorized access to restricted areas.
- Repeated violation of hostel rules.
- Disruptive behavior during classes or university events.
- Unauthorized commercial activity on campus.

**Category C — Minor Violations:**
- Violation of hostel timings.
- Failure to maintain cleanliness in shared spaces.
- Minor damage to university property.
- Violation of quiet hours.

### 4.2 Penalties

**Category A violations** may result in:
- Suspension for one (1) to four (4) semesters.
- Expulsion from the university.
- Forfeiture of hostel accommodation.
- Criminal complaint where applicable.
- Notation on the academic transcript.

**Category B violations** may result in:
- Written warning.
- Community service of 20 to 40 hours.
- Fine of ₹5,000 to ₹20,000.
- Temporary suspension of hostel privileges (up to 30 days).

**Category C violations** may result in:
- Verbal or written warning.
- Fine of up to ₹2,000.
- Temporary restriction on hostel privileges (up to 7 days).

### 4.3 Disciplinary Hearing Procedure

1. The Disciplinary Committee shall notify the student of the charges within five (5) working days of receiving a complaint.
2. The student shall be given at least ten (10) working days to prepare a response.
3. The student may be accompanied by a faculty advisor (not a legal representative) during the hearing.
4. The committee shall reach a decision within fifteen (15) working days of the hearing.
5. The decision shall be communicated in writing, including the rationale and any imposed penalty.

### 4.4 Suspension During Investigation

For Category A violations involving safety concerns, the Dean of Student Affairs may impose an **interim suspension** pending investigation. During interim suspension:
- The student must vacate hostel accommodation within 24 hours.
- The student may not enter campus premises without written permission.
- Academic deadlines and examinations missed during suspension shall be accommodated upon reinstatement if the student is found not responsible.

## 5. Health and Safety

### 5.1 Medical Facilities

The University Health Center operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends. Emergency medical services are available 24/7 through the campus ambulance service (extension 1100).

### 5.2 Insurance

All enrolled students are covered under the University Group Health Insurance Plan. The annual premium of ₹3,000 is included in the student activity fee. The plan covers:
- Hospitalization expenses up to ₹2,00,000 per year.
- Outpatient consultations at the University Health Center.
- Emergency ambulance services.
- Mental health counseling (up to 12 sessions per year).

### 5.3 Fire Safety

Every hostel building is equipped with fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, and sprinkler systems. Fire drills are conducted once per semester. Obstruction of fire exits, tampering with fire safety equipment, or filing false fire alarms is a Category A violation.

## 6. Anti-Ragging Policy

### 6.1 Zero Tolerance

Greenfield University maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form, as mandated by the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions.

### 6.2 Definition

Ragging includes any act that causes or is likely to cause physical or psychological harm, apprehension, shame, or embarrassment to a student, including but not limited to:
- Teasing, abusing, or playing practical jokes.
- Forcing a student to perform any act against their will.
- Any act of financial extortion or forceful expenditure.
- Any act of physical or sexual abuse.

### 6.3 Anti-Ragging Committee

The Anti-Ragging Committee consists of:
- Dean of Student Affairs (Chair).
- Two senior faculty members.
- Two representatives from non-teaching staff.
- Three student representatives (one from each year).
- One representative from civil society.

### 6.4 Reporting

Reports of ragging may be submitted through:
- The University Anti-Ragging Helpline (extension 1199).
- The online complaint form on the University Portal.
- In person to any member of the Anti-Ragging Committee.
- The National Anti-Ragging Helpline (1800-180-5522).

All complaints are treated as confidential. The identity of the complainant shall not be disclosed without their consent.
